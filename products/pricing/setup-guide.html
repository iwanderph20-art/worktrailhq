🏷️ ListPrice
Reseller Pricing Calculator · Quick Setup Guide

Thanks for your purchase! ListPrice is a single file that runs on any device — no install, no internet, no account. You'll be pricing listings in under two minutes.

Get started

1
Open the app.

Double-tap pricing.html on any phone, tablet, or computer. It opens right in your browser.

2
Add your brand (optional).

Tap the ⚙ button (top-right) to set your shop name, upload your logo, and pick an accent color. Tap Save.

3
Pick your marketplace.

Choose Etsy, eBay, Amazon, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, or Facebook/Shopify — the selling fee and fixed fee fill in automatically. Selling on somewhere else? Choose Custom and type your own rates.

Price a listing

›

Enter your costs: what you paid for the item, plus packaging/supplies and any shipping you cover.

›

Choose a target: a profit margin %, a markup on cost %, or a flat profit in dollars. The Recommended list price updates instantly, along with your net profit, margin, and ROI.

›

Read the receipt. The breakdown shows exactly where every dollar goes — costs, selling fees, ads, and the profit you keep.

Check a price you already have

›

Scroll to “Have a price in mind?” and type any listing price. ListPrice shows the profit and margin you'd actually keep at that price — or warns you in red if you'd lose money.

Save & export

›

Tap Save to name and store a calculation (great for a batch of items). Open Saved anytime to search your items or tap ⬇ CSV to download a spreadsheet for your records.

About the fee presets. The built-in marketplace rates are close estimates that combine selling and payment-processing fees. Marketplaces change these from time to time — the fee fields are fully editable, so set them to your current rates for the most accurate price.
Where is my data? Everything is stored privately in the browser on the device you use — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Export a CSV now and then as your own backup, and avoid “clear browsing data” for this page.
Tip — keep it one tap away. On iPad/Android, open pricing.html in your browser and choose Add to Home Screen for a clean, full-screen app you can open while you source and list.
